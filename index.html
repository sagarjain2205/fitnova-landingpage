01 / THE CHALLENGE

Good training needs
good guidance.

Getting consistent, useful feedback during a workout can be harder than it should be.

A / IN A BUSY GYM

One coach. Many people.

When a trainer is helping several people at once, it can be difficult to get a form check on every rep and every set.

B / TRAINING ALONE

It is easy to miss your form.

Without a spotter or coach nearby, you may not notice that your posture or movement needs attention.

C / TOO MUCH ADVICE

Online tips can conflict.

Advice varies from video to video. It can be tough to turn general tips into useful cues for the movement you are doing right now.

D / ACCESS TO GUIDANCE

One-to-one help takes planning.

Appointments and personal coaching are not always available at the exact moment you want to practise. A simple workout check-in can help fill that gap.

FitNova offers general exercise feedback and is not a substitute for a qualified trainer, physiotherapist, or medical advice.
